# P42: Action labels for Kerim's recordings

Every finished recording of Kerim's play can now be labelled as it comes in: `pilot/tools/label_actions.py` writes one line per stretch of one action into `actions.jsonl`, `--index` gathers everything labelled so far into a data set of two samples a second with his controller at that moment, and `lab/actions/process-new.sh` does both for every finished recording that has no labels yet. The eleven recordings of 11 November, 148.4 minutes over Wi-Fi, took 616 s in one process while the live recorder ran beside it, 4.2 s per minute of recording, so an hour takes about four minutes. Over those minutes 6.2 percent is left `unknown`. Looked at on contact sheets, the driving, walking, aiming, firing and fist-fight labels show what they say on 14 to 20 of 20 stretches; `idle` and `look` are weaker, and a bicycle is labelled as walking.

## The actions and how each is decided

The list is closed and has eighteen entries: `follow_route`, `drive_free`, `reverse_or_turn`, `enter_vehicle`, `leave_vehicle`, `walk`, `sprint`, `aim`, `fire`, `melee`, `look`, `cover`, `use_phone`, `menu`, `map`, `wait`, `idle` and `unknown`. The first group maps onto what the pilot's skills carry out (`drive_route`, `enter_vehicle`, `walk`, `walk_to`, `look`, `fight`, `melee`, `punch`, `wait`, `set_waypoint` on the map); the others are what a player visibly does besides.

The screen comes first, because a menu takes no ordinary input. The HUD reader's class decides `map`, `use_phone`, `menu` (pause, interaction menu, weapon wheel) and `wait` (loading, a mission passed or failed, wasted, busted). A game screen without a minimap for a second is a cutscene or a scene without HUD: `wait` while the hand is quiet, `unknown` while it is busy. A section of P38 that says cutscene, loading or banner also gives `wait` when `--sections` is passed; the tool works without it.

Then the figure's mode, on foot or in a vehicle, because R2 is a shot on foot and the accelerator in a car. A flat minimap that lasts a second puts the figure on foot. A tilted one does not put it into a vehicle by itself: in the alleys of Vespucci the reader calls the map tilted for six seconds at a time with Franklin running, which the first contact sheet showed. Getting in is a press of the enter button (Triangle in the profile) after which the map turns over within eight seconds; where stars keep the map tilted on foot, R2 held for half a second without a sprint after the press confirms it instead. The stretch from the press to the turn is `enter_vehicle`. Getting out is the same button followed by a flat map within four seconds, or by a quiet pedal for a second and a half and then a stick, and is `leave_vehicle`. A tilted map without a press counts only when it lasts five seconds with R2 held for a second in it, which is how a recording that starts in a car or a cutscene that ends in one is taken. A press after which nothing changes is no entry.

In a vehicle: L2 held for 0.6 s or longer without R2 is `reverse_or_turn`, a shorter one a brake inside the drive. R2, L2, a map that moves, or a release of R2 within the last 1.5 s while the map's motion cannot be read, is driving: `follow_route` when at least four in ten clean readings within a second either side show a route, `drive_free` when they do not, `unknown` when every reading there is damaged. A standing car with the hand at rest is `idle`, with only the right stick `look`; a car whose motion cannot be read long after the last R2 is `unknown`.

On foot, in this order: R2 pulled is `fire`, a press of the melee button (Circle) `melee`, L2 held `aim`, a press of the cover button (R1) `cover` for half a second, L1 held with the stick still `menu` (the weapon wheel the screen reader missed), the left stick at 0.5 or more with the sprint button (Cross) tapped within half a second `sprint`, the left stick at 0.2 or more `walk`, the right stick alone at 0.35 or more `look`, the hand at rest `idle`. Buttons held without a stick (Square, the D-pad, Cross alone) are `unknown`. The buttons for sprint, getting in, melee and cover come from the game profile, so `--game gta6` reads sprint as held L3; aim on L2, fire on R2 and the wheel on L1 are the GTA V layout and are not in the profile.

Two rules work over neighbouring samples. A flicker shorter than 0.3 s between two stretches of the same ongoing action joins them, which keeps a sprint in one piece between taps; a press, `unknown` and anything the screen decided are never smoothed away. And a rest of the hand shorter than a second between two inputs is `unknown`, not `idle`: on the held-out sheet of `20261111-182316` such pauses were fist fights going on between punches, a bicycle rolling on and a gun being lowered. Where no controller is in the recording (`pad.jsonl` with at most one line), only the screen decides and everything else is `unknown`; a hand that was never seen is no hand at rest. A controller state that is not neutral and has not changed for twenty seconds is a controller lying on its sticks and gives `unknown`.

The decision is one function, `actions.decide(sample, context, layout)`, and `label` takes any function of that shape, so the judge's answers can replace it later.

## The controller, event by event, and its lead

`pad.jsonl` is read line by line and never sampled: every sample of ten a second owns the window of hand time up to the next sample and collects the highest stick and trigger values in it, the time the triggers were held, and every press that began in it, a line on the window's first instant included. A tap of 30 ms between two samples is therefore in the label, which a test pins.

His hand leads the picture. The camera turns when the right stick is pushed, so every recording carries its own lead: the tool measures the horizontal motion of the middle of the picture between samples by phase correlation and finds the shift at which the right stick and that motion agree best, between 0 and 1.5 s in steps of 0.02 s. On the eleven recordings it found 0.14 to 0.18 s, correlation 0.30 to 0.45; the last, short one (0.27) fell below the bar of 0.3 and took the default of 0.20 s, which is the first measurement on `20261111-164039`. So over this Wi-Fi the lead is about what the cable gave on 10 November, not the 0.6 to 1.1 s of the Wi-Fi recording of that morning. The frame at time t is labelled with the hand of the window that starts at t less the lead; labels, first and last frame and all times stay on the clock of `frames.jsonl`. `--lead` overrides the estimate, and `labels.json` beside every `actions.jsonl` records the lead, its source and its correlation.

## The data set

`label_actions.py --index DIR` writes `DIR/index.jsonl` over every folder that has `actions.jsonl` and `labels.json`: per sample, two a second by the frame times, the recording, frame index, time, action and the controller state at that time less the lead, which is the hand that caused the frame. It copies no pixels. `DIR/summary.md` holds minutes and stretches per action and per recording; the one for the eleven recordings is committed in `lab/actions/out/summary.md`, the rest of `lab/actions/out/` (4.8 MB) is ignored and rebuilt. `actions.training_frames(index, actions=..., limit=..., seed=...)` yields `(frame, label)` with the frame as 1280x720 RGB, reading each video once from the front. `lab/actions/check_reader.py` drew 12 of the 1767 samples of `20261111-164039` through it in 14.8 s; all 12 came back at the right size and equal to the frame found by seeking, and the sheet showed each label fitting its frame.

`lab/actions/process-new.sh [RECORDINGS_ROOT] [OUTPUT_ROOT]` (defaults `/Users/kerim/recordings/kerim` and `/Users/kerim/recordings/derived/kerim`) labels every folder whose `meta.json` has `"seconds"` and which has no `actions.jsonl` in the output root, in one process, then refreshes the index; `--recordings-root` lets the index find a recording by name once it has moved to another disk. It was tried on a scratch root of links to `20261111-192328`, `-200111`, `-203117` and `-205309`: the first run labelled the three finished ones in 69 s, a second run found nothing new, and `-205309`, then being recorded, was left alone. `actions.jsonl` is written last and renamed into place, so a folder that has it is complete. Nothing was written under `/Users/kerim/recordings/`.

For `20261111-164039` I looked at every action with ten stretches or more. The rules were changed twice after a first look at these sheets, for the alley case and for getting in, so the counts below are not blind; for that reason four actions were also looked at on `20261111-182316`, which no rule had been tuned on, and `wait` on `20261111-171046`, where `20261111-164039` has only six.

On `20261111-164039`: `follow_route` 15 of 15 (all there are), a driven car with the route on the minimap. `drive_free` 19 of 20; the one is a car with its doors still open at the end of getting in. `walk` 18 of 20 on foot and moving, almost all at a jog, since a full stick without Cross jogs; one figure lies on the ground and one stands over a body. `sprint` 18 of 20 running; the two others are inside the garage, where the game lets him only walk. `aim` 18 of 20 with the gun raised, `fire` 18 of 20 with the gun raised and in several the muzzle flash; the other two of each show no gun at all. `idle` 14 of 15 (all there are) a figure or a car at rest or the game's own idle camera, one figure moving. `look` cannot be checked from a single frame, since the turn is what it claims; 16 of 20 show a figure standing or crouching as a look would, two show him climbing a fence, one a car the motion reader called standing while it drove, one a shot. `cover`, 11 stretches, shows him in or going into cover on about six; R1 pressed in the open does nothing visible. ## What the controller alone cannot tell

Whether a shot was aimed at an enemy, hit anyone, or was fired at all: R2 on foot without a weapon drawn shows nothing, and an aimed L2 says nothing of the target. Whether a punch or kick landed. Whether a press of R1 found a wall to take cover at, and when he left cover, which happens by moving the stick away. Whether L2 in a car brakes or reverses. Whether the car moves at all after the pedal is released, for which the tool needs the map's motion and has it on only some frames. Walking from jogging beyond the stick's magnitude, and a sprint from a walk where an interior forbids running. A bicycle from on foot, a motorbike or boat from a car, the driver's seat from a passenger's. Whether the enter button got him into a vehicle, which only the map turning over says. Whether a rest of the hand is waiting for a dialogue, watching, or a fight animation going on. A cutscene from a scene the game plays without HUD while he could act, such as the dog sequence. And which way he looks: the right stick names the turn, not what he turned towards.

## What was not verified and what to know

No frame was labelled by hand, so there is no accuracy number beyond the sheets, and one still frame cannot show a turn of the camera or the difference between a walk and a jog. The contact sheets show only the middle frame of each stretch. The bicycle is a known fault of the mode: a triangle press without a turning map leaves the figure on foot, and in `20261111-182316` that makes about 40 s of cycling into walk and sprint. The minimap's motion on foot calls a figure at rest moving too often to be used there, and in a car it sometimes calls a moving car standing. The detector of a missing minimap does not tell a cutscene from gameplay without HUD. The lead's correlation is weak (0.30 to 0.45) though its top is broad and lands within 0.06 s on every recording; over a worse Wi-Fi it may need `--lead`. Sections of P38 were not passed in this run, since that branch is not merged; reading them is covered by the code path and not by a measurement. The GTA V buttons for aim, fire and the weapon wheel are the standard layout and not measured on this console.
