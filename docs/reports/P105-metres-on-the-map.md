The method tracks road-texture features, rejects bad forward/backward matches and homography outliers, then finds an inverse projection under which road points move by a planar similarity. It estimates X = a*s*x/(1-k*y), Y = s*y/(1-k*y), with x right and y ahead relative to the arrow. Full-data geometry gives k = 0.0065 per pixel and a = 0.575; median similarity residual is 7.26% of inter-frame translation. A clip's GPS decrease, corrected by logged fix age, supplies metres; accumulated texture displacement is corrected for measured inter-frame zoom. Missing endpoint speeds/fixes split a clip rather than bridge a GPS jump. Feature correspondences, translations, zoom factors and fix ages are preserved in p105-motion-points.json and p105-motion.json.
