The rule. A box is in the car's lane when its centre lies within 0.7 of the own car's width (as the car looks at the box's depth) from the middle of the picture; the box's edge is not used because a lorry in the next lane overlaps the jeep's side in the picture all the way, and a lorry beside the car has a box of half the picture with its centre far out. Anything not in the lane has no say, which is what leaves the lorries beside the jeep alone. In the lane, a thing is followed from one detection to the next and the height of its box over the last second says how fast it comes closer: it is gone round only if the height grows by 20 percent a second or more (about four seconds to contact) and the history is at least half a second long. That pushes as before, with the same side keeping and the same pedal. A thing in the lane that does not grow, or whose history is still short, gets no sideways push; the pedal is lifted from a nearness of 0.35 (about 15 m) to 0.1 of the pedal at nearness 1, and the brake of `urgent` is kept only for a thing seen for the first time very close. The first look at a thing says nothing about its speed, so for the first half second it is only followed; at 10 m/s a standing thing seen at 25 m or more leaves the room, but one that appears within about 5 m gets its sideways push half a second late. That is the price of the rule and the first run should watch for it.

Two boxes are the same thing when both are of one kind (a person against any vehicle, but car, truck, bus and motorcycle count as one), the new centre lies within 0.6 of the old box's larger side and the height is between 0.6 and 1.7 times the old one. A thing that gets no box is remembered for 0.7 s and its history continues when it comes back; after that it starts again. The growth is read from the height only, so the detector calling a lorry a truck, a bus and a car in turn does not change it; the name that was given most often in the last eight detections is only used to size the nearness. The tests show a lorry called by three names with a lost frame as steady, and a growing one with the same changes as growing. Detections are matched once per detection, not once per tick, because the same boxes arrive on several ticks.

Parts of the own car: a vehicle box in the middle of the picture (within 0.06 of its width of the centre), at least 0.13 of the width wide and at least 2.2 times as wide as tall is left out. That is a flat shape that a car ahead seen from behind never has (it is about as tall as wide); the jeep seen over a bonnet in the collision has it. It is a guess made from one event of four boxes. A car crossing in front, seen from the side and close, has the same shape and would be left out too. I kept it in `avoid.py` and did not touch `is_player` in `objects.py`, which other code relies on. `waits_behind` and `passing_side` are as they were and still see such boxes. The `bypass` path keeps the old geometry and the old commitment to the free side; its test in `test_player_driving.py` needed a history of the car coming closer before the normal step, because the normal step now asks for one.

Tests. `pilot/tests/test_avoid.py` was changed for the new rule: the old tests that pushed on the first look at a thing now feed it a second of growth first, which is the case the rule keeps; none was weakened otherwise. New tests: a steady car ahead is followed and the pedal lifted; growing things are gone round, including a slow one at 25 percent and not one at 8 percent; the steadiness against a lost frame and a changed label, and a thing lost for longer starting over; a lorry beside the car with a box of half the picture; the flat own-car box; the bypass; and three replays of the fixture's boxes (no push above 0.1 in all four stretches, the pedal lifted 24.9 to 25.3 s behind the lorry, nothing for the jeep's own boxes at 216.3 s).
