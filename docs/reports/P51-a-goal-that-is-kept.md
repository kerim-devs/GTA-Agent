# P51: a goal that is kept

The free-roam head now keeps one bounded goal across ordinary head requests. Its round takes a vehicle, drives to a far waypoint, gets out to punch a visible person, escapes the police and starts again. With no vehicle in view it first seeks a road. The implementation is local to the card's write set. There was no console, daemon, Remote Play, model or network call, no recording was changed, and no Git state was written. The reviewer owns committing and integration under `docs/tasks/README.md`.

| Goal | Start condition | Existing skills / steps | Observed reach condition | Limit | Successor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicle | On foot with a visible car, truck or motorcycle, or after the road goal | `enter_vehicle`; `walk_road` while waiting for a vehicle | Accepted vehicle movement mode after the active entry skill finishes its confirmation | 90 seconds or 3 issued attempts | Drive |
| Drive | Already in a vehicle, or vehicle goal reached | `set_waypoint away` with seeded cardinal direction and `distance=far`, then `drive_route`; continue an observed route | Completed `drive_route` with `route-gone`/`arrived`, with a route observed during this goal or by that running skill | 240 seconds or 3 attempts | Fight |
| Fight | Drive reached, or drive gave up while still in a vehicle | `press triangle`, short `wait`, observe foot mode; look / walk to seek a person, then `punch` | Completed punch skill, which reports pulses in reach or a target remaining down | 90 seconds or 4 attempts | Escape |
| Escape | Wanted stars preempt any other goal, or after the fight | Existing wanted rules: vehicle entry, far escape waypoint and driving, or sprinting on foot | Wanted was observed positive during this goal and is now explicitly zero | 180 seconds or 4 attempts | Vehicle |
| Road | On foot without a nearby vehicle | `walk_road`; existing failure recovery can look and walk when there is no road or turn when stuck | Runner reports the player on a road for at least 0.3 seconds, a vehicle becomes visible, or movement is already in a vehicle | 90 seconds or 4 attempts | Vehicle |

Initial free-roam selection requires a known movement mode in gameplay, no screen objective or retained mission intent, and no interior red, yellow or blue marker. Existing marker rules remain in charge until then. Once a goal is active, incidental markers and temporarily unreadable movement do not replace its useful running work. A limit includes time waiting for head responses. An issued retry consumes the attempt budget; ordinary `go_on` answers do not. A failed goal is not immediately selected again. If an escape goal expires while stars remain, the existing police rules keep control without immediately starting another escape goal. Missing wanted readings never count as clearing the police. Route disappearance without a previously observed route never counts as arrival. Punch completion proves the existing skill's observed/pulsed outcome, not independently measured damage.

The seed is generated once per head, logged on every goal answer and accepted explicitly by `MissionHead(goal_seed=...)` for replay. It chooses among the four existing far-waypoint directions. Seed 51 is fixed in the checked-in replay. Goal, step, action and the reason for keeping or changing the plan travel through the validated response contract into `head.jsonl`. The single free-roam handover leaves the existing mission and failure/blip rule blocks intact. Existing recovery plans are reused inside active goals. All 15 frozen cases with an objective retain their previous rule and plan.

`go_on` has an empty plan and never calls `replace` or `append`. It retains the exact active skill and queue, including on `plan_ending`, and preserves epoch rejection of overtaken answers. If a skill finishes while a keep answer is in flight, the conductor requests the next step immediately instead of waiting for the quiet timeout. An expected movement-mode change keeps the entry skill alive until it finishes confirmation, and an unreadable mode is not a replacement reason. A waypoint skill keeps ownership of its own map until it closes it. Stars, health below one third, death/arrest, an objective or the goal deadline stop goal-owned work before the next skill tick, even before the flow handler can return early. A `goal_end` row records that interruption.

The offline command is `pilot/.venv/bin/python lab/goals/replay.py`. It loads the pre-change rules from local commit `b396957`, reads recording logs without modification, runs one old and one new head per session in request order and writes [the full replay](../../lab/goals/p51-replay.json) and [the comparison with every changed case](../../lab/goals/p51-replay.md). Inputs have SHA-256 receipts in the JSON. The corpus contains 173 mission cases, 18 take-out cases, one P45 changed case and 51 P45 session requests. All 82 changes have no screen objective: 65 now use the road goal, 15 the drive goal and two the vehicle goal. The comparison explains each individual change.
