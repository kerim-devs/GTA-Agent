`Flow.tick(observation, text)` returns `None` during ordinary play or an expected menu window. Otherwise it returns `FlowResult(pad, action, events)`, taking control immediately with neutral input while confirming a screen. Two consecutive observations with increasing timestamps confirm entry and exit. A one-tick wrong screen never generates a press. Missing HUD data after a warning or during an active screen keeps controls neutral and restarts confirmation. Repeated or backwards timestamps cannot confirm a screen. Every emitted pad has zero sticks and triggers. The only supported pressed buttons are Cross and Circle; no shoulder button is ever generated.

Failure retry requires a readable retry/button pair for two observations. This is deliberate: the recording shows a failure banner almost three seconds before the retry footer becomes available. The weapon wheel is released with neutral input. Passed, loading, wasted and busted wait neutrally. An unchanged screen receives at most one button pulse, followed by a picture check and a two-second response deadline. Waiting screens have a 30-second deadline. A stuck screen stays neutral and emits its event once; it never guesses another button. Recognized screen changes start a new sequence, which permits map → pause → game with one verified Circle per level.

Checked in the night of 10 to 11 November. The default gate passes. Fourteen breaks were tried and every one fails a test: acting on a screen or a button read once, pressing twice on the same screen, closing an expected menu, an expectation that never expires, pressing circle on the failed screen without its footer, never reporting stuck, pressing on the passed banner, L1 on the weapon wheel, a failure count that survives a pass, handing control back when the HUD reading is lost, a repeated timestamp, taking over ordinary play, and repeating the failure event.

One change. A press lasted a single tick, 33 ms at the loop's rate, while Kerim's own presses in the recording last about 140 ms; a press that short is easy for the console to miss, and then the reflex reports a stuck screen after one unanswered attempt. It is held for three ticks now, still once per screen, and let go at once when the HUD reading is lost. Two tests pin that and the simulation counts a press where it starts.

The frame-by-frame finding that the retry footer appears three seconds after the failed banner, and that Kerim's first four presses of cross went into nothing, is the kind of thing this card was for. The retry depends on the text reader seeing the footer; the conductor recognises text twice a second, which is enough.
