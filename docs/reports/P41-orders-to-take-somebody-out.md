The rules head now answers the game's order to take somebody out. On foot, with an objective that orders a kill and at least one red blip inside the minimap, `MissionHead` plans one `fight` of 20 seconds without cover under the rule `take_out_fight`. Everything else it decided before it still decides the same way, which the comparison on Kerim's recorded play below confirms step by step.

The order is recognised by its verb. The game writes it as "Schalte <somebody> aus.", and over Wi-Fi the verb survives better than the rest: the recordings hold "Schalte dle Vagos aus.", "Schalte die V8gos aus.", "Schalte den GafUI", "Schalte den Gartne" and "Schalte den Gai ti ie", the last three without the closing "aus". The pattern `TAKE_OUT` in `pilot/src/pilot/mission.py` therefore matches the word "Schalte" on its own, after the same case and accent folding the head already applies, and tolerates the confusions seen in those texts: 8 for e and a, i or 1 for l, 5 for s. An objective that ends in "ein" or "an" switches something on and is not taken for an order. Only the objective line is read, never the help text, which on the same frames reads "Halte LI gedrückt, um das Waffenrad aufzurufen" and similar. Words that merely contain the letters, such as "Autowaschanlage" or "einzuschalten", do not match because the verb has to stand as a word of its own. Other wordings of a kill order, such as "Töte" or "Erledige", appear in none of the recordings and are not recognised.

The rule sits where the head already handled red blips on foot, behind every rule that must win over it. Screens are answered first. Stars send the figure away and are never fought, low health below a third gets away first, and a failed skill is followed by a walk before anything is tried again, so a fight that timed out is not repeated at once. In a vehicle the route rules stay as they were. These are the boundaries `head_prompt.txt` sets for combat: fight only red blips in a mission, never with stars, and stop when health is low. The duration of 20 seconds keeps the fight bounded below the skill's own limit of 30, so the rules look at the objective again at least that often; Kerim's first attempt at the Vagos lasted 23 seconds before it failed. Stars and low health that appear during a fight still reach the head as events, and the conductor replaces the running plan with the answer.

When no red blip is in range the rule does not act, and the existing rules decide. This is deliberate. `fight` turns towards the nearest red blip and reports done after two seconds without one, so planning it on an empty map would end at once and ask again. Looking around does not help either: the minimap shows the same blips around the figure whichever way the camera faces, so turning the camera reveals no enemy on it. What brings enemies into range is walking towards what the game marks, and the existing `foot_target` rule does exactly that with `walk_to` on a yellow or blue blip, bounded at ten seconds. With nothing marked at all, the picture is the only source left, and the request goes to the model behind the rules, or to the default walk without one. A red blip pinned to the map's border also leaves the decision to the existing rules: its distance is unknown and `fight` would fire along its bearing at something out of sight.

`pilot/tests/test_mission.py` pins this. The four recorded objective steps that hold the order and a red blip in range plan exactly one fight. Eight misread or rewritten objectives, among them the five recorded readings, plan a fight on the recorded Vagos frame, and nine other objectives on the same frame, among them "Begib dich zu der Autowaschanlage.", "Bring das Auto zum Autohaus.", "Schalte das Radio ein." and the help text, still reach the model unchanged. Every recorded step that does not hold the order decides the same rule P39 recorded for it. With one or three stars, with and without a car in view, no fight, melee, punch or shoot is planned. Low health, read from the HUD or only from the event, walks away first, and the recorded first Vagos frame at a third of the health stays `low_health_on_foot`. Without a red in range the order walks to a yellow or blue blip, and a border red or an empty map reaches the model. In a vehicle the order changes nothing, and a failed fight is followed by a walk. With the old `mission.py` put back, twelve of these tests fail, all of them the new decision and its misreadings; the boundary tests pass on both, as they should.

The 20 seconds are a choice, not a measurement.
