## Review

Checked in the night of 10 to 11 November.

`fight` is sound for what it can see: it turns the way the walking skills turn (the same angle from the same blip coordinates as `face`), attacks only with a readable red blip in front, lets go at once when the map is damaged, tilted or gone, and never calls a fight won without two seconds of a readable map without red. Twenty-one breaks were tried and nineteen failed a test. One is guarded twice, the reset of the clear time after a long gap. One was not noticed and is pinned now: the trigger held for the whole lock instead of pulsed.

Two small changes. The argument is `duration` as in every other skill and both skills expose their `limit`, which is where the runner looks to know how long a plan still runs. `stop` has a default reason like the other skills.

`melee` is not a fighting skill yet and the report says so: it clears three enemies in 23 of 100 simulated runs, it does not walk to its target, and it gives up when the nearest red blip is more than 18 map pixels away.

The bigger limit is what both skills look at. Red blips are the enemies of a mission. Someone the character meets on the street has no blip, and police show differently, so outside missions `fight` ends after two seconds with nothing to do, which is harmless, and nobody gets hit. Hitting whoever stands in front of the character needs the people the object detector finds, and comes as its own card once that detector is in.

Open, all of it on the console: whether the stick values turn the camera at all (the smallest is 0.16 and the game has a dead zone), whether L2 locks onto the enemy the minimap says is in front, the cadence, and what R1 does without a wall nearby. Assisted aim has to be set to full in the game. Not registered yet.
