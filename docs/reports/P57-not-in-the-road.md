`foot.py` measures the arrow against both edges of a local white minimap band. Four cross sections outside the arrow must agree on a 4–18 pixel strip; crossings, inconsistent edges, clipped sections and unreadable maps abstain. It steers outward from a lane, follows the road outside the band, prevents a guard escape from steering back across the edge, and reserves the final two seconds for stopping at the side.
