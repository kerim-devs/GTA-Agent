P57 teaches `walk_road` to leave a carriageway the map shows the figure in, and `enter_vehicle` to take a vehicle within reach before one that is not, to pass over one it has seen a driver in, and not to stand in traffic for vehicles it will not take. It does not establish safe roadside arrival in the recorded night scene. The review of the first version found that it stood still wherever the map could not tell the side, which is most of the time; the repair below makes the map's silence mean "walk as before and say so". A console trial and the conductor and rules integration remain necessary before relying on it in traffic.

`foot.py` measures the arrow against both edges of a local white minimap band (`_road_edges`). Four cross sections outside the arrow must agree on a 4 to 18 pixel strip; a crossing under the arrow, disagreeing edges, a clipped section and an unreadable map abstain, and so does a white sheet (car park, plaza, wide junction) within 12 pixels of the arrow, which the band reader has already cut away and which says nothing about a kerb. A reading is `lane` (the arrow inside the band or less than 2 px outside its edge), `side`, or `unknown`. It measures a map band, not a pavement; translucent terrain, intersections and perspective remain limits, and no world-image kerb detector was added. It reads a lane or a side on about one foot tick in nine with a road in sight (572 of 6,448 sampled foot frames of ten human recordings; 367 of the 3,305 with the arrow within 11 px of a band), so the walk is built to work without it. Only a positive `lane` reading makes `walk_road` leave the carriageway: outward to the nearer edge, kept for 1.5 s so that one tick the map cannot read does not turn the figure back, and a way round of the guard that would turn toward the carriageway is not taken while the edge is known and near. Where the side is unknown it walks exactly as the walk before P57 did, to the road and along it by the same road direction, then on a compass heading when the road is gone, and carries `side unknown` in its reason; a `side` reading with the band's edge more than 11 px away keeps it walking toward the road. The only stand-still the map causes is a map that cannot be read before any heading exists, 1.5 s and then `map-unreadable`. It ends at its time limit: `done` with `road-side (map band; kerb unverified)` only on a positive side reading with the edge near for 0.3 s, `failed` with `still-in-lane` on a lane reading, and otherwise `done` as before, with `time-limit; side unknown` (or `time-limit; roadside not reached` when the side was known and far, or plain `time-limit` when no road was ever on the map). The runner's `road_reached` (read by the goals) is kept whether or not the edges could be read.

`vehicle_entry.py` accepts `occupied: "avoid" | "allow"`, defaulting to `avoid`; the generic head argument parser and schema pick it up. `choose_vehicle` ranks a vehicle seen moving last; among the others one within reach before one that is not; then the evidence (seen standing for a whole 0.4 s span with no person box in it, then unknown, then a person box confirmed in it), standing, a car before a bus, the nearer. The person box (a contained upper-body box, with three pixels of tolerance for box jitter) is a hint, not proof: it lowers a vehicle and, seen on two detections 0.1 s apart, makes `avoid` pass it over; it is never the only reason to stand and wait, and its absence is not "empty" (the detector misses drivers). `parked-candidate` is claimed only for a vehicle seen standing across a whole 0.4 s span with no person box in it; one seen moving and later stopped is traffic and counts as occupied. `_search` stands still only for a vehicle it may still take and cannot yet judge (a person box just seen; a far one at the roadside not yet seen standing), at most 3 s per vehicle. A vehicle that moves, one it has rejected (locked) and one it avoids hold nothing: the search goes on as before, a camera scan and then the road walk. After an unconfirmed press with a lane reading it retreats after one second; with no lane reading there is no retreat. When time runs out the reason says what it saw (`saw 3 vehicles (rejected 1, avoided 1, moving 1)`), and success reports the detector's vehicle type, the occupancy evidence (`unknown`, `parked-candidate`, `occupied`) and the road reading. `avoid` passes over a car whose driver was seen; it is not a guarantee of an empty car. `head_prompt.txt` no longer calls taking an occupied car fine.

The rules should call `enter_vehicle {"occupied":"avoid"}` when health is low or a mission runs, and may use `allow` only for an intentional occupied-vehicle attempt with its retaliation risk accepted. They must not turn `unknown` into `empty`. The conductor must remove the unconditional opening levelling and waiting in a lane, obtain current roadside evidence before a stationary action, and treat a `walk_road` ending with `side unknown` as an unverified location, not a roadside. `mission.py` still plans on a `no-road` reason that nothing produces, and a `failed` `walk_road` (`still-in-lane`, `stuck`) falls to the `failed_skill` rule, `[_walk()]`, which walks straight ahead from wherever the figure stands. The goals' `road` goal ends on `road_reached`, "road observed under player", which is the state this change tries to avoid as an end; it should end on a `done` walk whose reason starts `road-side` or, failing that, ask the conductor for a look at the world picture.
