`prompts.py` returns a typed offer with button, press/hold, seconds, matched wording and an explicit known/guess flag. It normalizes the observed OCR substitutions and refuses phone, internet, save, stock-market, property-purchase, GTA Online and PlayStation instructions before looking in the table. Unknown offers use the active profile's new context binding, `right`. Confirm and back reuse the existing profile bindings. Glyph recognition is disabled: the six available crops comprise four right glyphs, one R1 and one square, with no independent sample sufficient to establish 90% accuracy.

`DoPrompt` and `UsePlace` each own a background TextReader and a single replaceable pending frame. The tick never runs OCR. Bad clocks, missing/corrupt frames and stale readings return neutral. Short presses are released on the next tick, including a 300 ms tick, without sending the button again. Holds use their requested duration. DoPrompt reports success only after observing changed or absent help text within two seconds. Failed OCR is an abstention, not evidence that the text disappeared.

UsePlace waits for a welcome without a minimap, searches in eight bounded walk/look phases, opens the offered interaction, reads menu rows and separately boxed prices, navigates a deterministic number of rows, optionally opens one recognized category, and confirms at most one priced item. `spend=no` never confirms. Prices over the class limit of $150, unread prices, and changed rows/prices are not confirmed. A purchase result requires a visible receipt; sending Cross alone is not called a purchase. Normal exits and readable failure paths back out until gameplay with a minimap is observed, then return a neutral pad. The scripted tests cover multiple menu levels and unread menus.
