# Recorded personal-car symbol

`pilot.own_vehicle.find_own_vehicle(crop)` reads the upright cyan front-car glyph in a 260 by 170 RGB uint8 HUD crop. It returns one `VehicleMarker(x, y, clipped, confidence)` or `None`. Coordinates are HUD pixels relative to the player arrow, right and up positive. `clipped` means that the game pins the marker to the radar rim; it does not mean that pixels are missing, and its position must not be used as a distance. Confidence is a shape correlation, not a probability. The reader does not select or enter any vehicle.

The template comes from `20261113-201617/head-0002.jpg`. The marker is associated with the car Kerim identified as his in `20261113-195746/head-0002.jpg` and followed in the recovery recordings beginning at `20261113-201348`; its ownership is supplied by that session context, not inferred from blue colour. Colour alone is insufficient: the padded silhouette and dark windscreen must both match. Multiple separated matches abstain. The arrow neighbourhood is excluded because the symbol is obscured there in the reported 19:57 failure. This reader therefore cannot solve that close-car case by itself. It recognises the recorded map glyph, not the ownership of a world-view object. Michael's cyan symbol is the only supported variant; other colours, motorcycles, damaged images and changed resolutions have not been validated.

The frozen fixture manifest records source paths, image hashes and visually inspected positions. The nine head-0001 frames were excluded from template and threshold selection. Six of their seven visible vehicle symbols are detected within two HUD pixels, with one conservative miss at 20:16:17, and neither of the two frames without a visible car symbol produces a match. These frames are from the same recovery trip as development frames, so this is a small frame holdout, not independent episode validation. The visual annotations were not confirmed by the user. Three earlier real minimap crops with ordinary blue markers, letters and player arrows produce no match; two overlapping 19:57 car/player crops also abstain. Synthetic duplication checks refusal of two candidate symbols and is not included in accuracy counts.

The minimal validation command is `PYTHONPATH=pilot/src /Users/kerim/Documents/ChatGPT/gta/pilot/.venv/bin/python -m pytest pilot/tests/test_own_vehicle.py`. No console inputs, model requests or video exports were used. Before wider use, collect independent evidence for other character colours and vehicle types, near-arrow overlap, damaged images and symbols moving onto every map edge. An absent detection must never be interpreted as an absent personal car.
