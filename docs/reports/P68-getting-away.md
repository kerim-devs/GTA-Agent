## The readings of the minimap

`pilot/src/pilot/terrain.py` reads, from the HUD crop alone, where roads cross two rings around the arrow (20 and 28 crop pixels), whether paint lies under the car, where water lies within the map in 10 degree bins with its distance, and, from the minimap reader's own blips, the bearings of red markers. Each returns None where it cannot say: no minimap or a damaged picture (`foot.crop_ok`), or a radar washed out by sun on concrete (the land's tenth percentile of value at 190 or above).
