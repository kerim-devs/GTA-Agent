Checked in the night of 10 to 11 November without a single sound: every run here was muted or had no voice at all. The rework does what the card asked. The loop keeps reading while a line is written, spoken or synthesised, one line is in the works at a time, chat that gets no answer gets silence with a reason, and the message to answer is marked apart from the other quotes.

The real writer, measured with `tools/commentator_latency.py` (`lab/commentator/p20b-live-latency.jsonl`): the first chat line takes 5.3 s with the start of the process in it, the nine after it 3.1 to 6.3 s. An event took 3.5 to 9.8 s through the character and never the two seconds it is given.

That makes the character the wrong default for events. Every event waited its two seconds, was then spoken as the plain line anyway, and the writer's process was killed to cut the late answer off, so the next chat message paid the cold start again. Events are now spoken plainly and at once unless `--event-writer character` asks for the character; the rotating plain lines are what the viewer hears. The first failure is no longer counted aloud ("Fehlschlag Nummer 1"), the count starts with the second. A failed `punch` and a failed `melee` have lines too, since both skills are in the registry now.
