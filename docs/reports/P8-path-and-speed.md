The steering half of this work stands. The speed half does not survive contact with the game. The vehicle map prints the distance to the destination, and against the speed taken from it the map speed stays between 5 and 9 pixels per second from 6 to 45 m/s, because the game zooms the map out as the car gets faster. A limit of 7 pixels per second therefore limits nothing, and the simulator's fixed scale and proportional sensor do not describe the game. The numbers are in `docs/tasks/P9-speed-from-gps-distance.md`, which replaces the pixel speed with the printed distance.

Merged as the base for that card. Not to be driven on the real car above walking pace.
