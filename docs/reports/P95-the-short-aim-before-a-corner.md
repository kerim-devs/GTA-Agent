# P95: The short aim before a corner

The card went to a Codex worker that stopped on its usage limit with an untested beginning; the reviewer took the card over and this is his report. The worker's fixture of the recorded readings, `pilot/tests/fixtures/p95-corner.json` (206 to 216 s of `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/20261113-183223`), is kept and used.

Both faults repeat. The driver replayed on the recorded readings holds the stick at zero from 207.9 to 210.0 s and then sends 0.42, 0.59 and 0.51 to the left before the right turn, as the console did. The reason is in the geometry of `_target`: the path read at 210.6 s is `(0,13) (2,18) (8,22) (15,22)`, its first segment leans 22 degrees into the turn, and the line that continues it back under the arrow passes the car 4.8 px to its left, so the point of that line 8 px from the car lies ahead and to the left. The continuation takes the hidden route for a straight line; here the route ran straight from the car to the path's first point and bent after it. And the short aim's swing on a straight is in `lab/drive/measured.py`: 6.3 to 7.2 degrees of spread without the turn's memory and 3.7 to 4.1 with it, at 8, 12 and 16 m/s.

Two changes, both in `drive.py`. Where the way is not straight enough for the far aim but the path's beginning goes on from the car, the aim is taken on the path itself, 14 px out (`bend_lookahead_px`, `_onward`). The beginning goes on from the car when its direction over 12 px differs by less than 25 degrees from the line to the path's first point, or from the car's own heading; once that holds it holds up to 33 degrees. A corner under the arrow fails both tests: for a right angle 6 px ahead the two directions differ by 33 and by 90 degrees. A car beside a straight route passes the second and is brought back by the middle aim, where the short one stood up to 60 degrees to the side. And where the short aim remains, its continuation under the arrow goes by way of the car's own direction: the hidden stretch is taken to run from the car along its heading to where that meets the continued line, and along the line from there, so that while the meeting point lies ahead and further off than the aim, the aim lies straight ahead. Where the two do not meet ahead of the car and before the path's first point, the continuation is as it was. For a right angle under the arrow this gives the same aim as before; for the bend of 210 s it gives straight ahead instead of 15 degrees to the left. The pedal still goes by the short aim alone, as before, and the comparison switch `PILOT_AIM_AB` gives its short-aim stretches the short aim alone.

The pedal is found and not mended. From 207.4 to 210.3 s the driver's own limit for the corner stood at 8.6 to 9.8 m/s and the car at 9.2 to 13.3. The throttle fell from 0.32 to zero as it should, but at 0.03 to 0.20 of the throttle the trigger was still sent at 0.37 to 0.48, because the profile lifts every pedal over the trigger's dead zone, and a jeep gains speed at that. The brake comes only 3 m/s over the limit (`brake_margin` in the bold style) and with 0.15 of the trigger for each metre a second beyond that, so 4.5 m/s over the limit braked with 0.03. It came with 0.49 at 211.1 s, in the corner, when the bend had dropped the limit to 7.3. A smaller margin or a pedal that is let go entirely under the limit changes how every drive brakes, and the pulses of the brake were tuned in P73 and P80 on recorded drives; that is a card of its own.

What the first run at the console has to watch: a right-angled turn at a junction approached at speed, for a stick that stays still and then turns the right way; a bend of a country road, for the aim's distance in the log (`aim_px` 14) and a line without the swing; and a car well beside its route after a way round something, for a flat return.
