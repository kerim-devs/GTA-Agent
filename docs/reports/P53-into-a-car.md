The duration is capped at 45 seconds instead of 25. A normal entry gets seven neutral seconds after the press, allowing the observed 5.3357-second human p90 and the existing one-second seated confirmation. A bus gets thirteen seconds because the recorded successful bus entry took 11.6 seconds before its seated event. Triangle is held for 0.15 seconds, close to the measured 0.1401-second human median. There are at most four presses. After a failed press the skill distinguishes a lost/departed target, insufficient reach, a possibly lost press, and a door that remains locked or blocked after three attempts; these are image-based diagnoses, not proof of a lock. A rejected vehicle is avoided while tracked and another candidate can be selected. Missing detections after the animation wait have their own five-second deadline. Reach is checked again on the fresh frame after the release tick, so a departing vehicle cannot trigger a stale press. Every terminal result is neutral.

The entire skill is uninterruptible, including approach and the release tick immediately before pressing. This protects a new plan arriving on either side of that boundary, at the cost of delaying replacement for up to 45 seconds. The current runner reads this attribute dynamically and the conductor appends rather than replaces while it is true. A property limited to tap/wait would be technically possible without modifying the runner. Before using that narrower policy, the conductor owner should test outstanding head requests across the approach-to-tap transition, queue clearing and emergency stop behavior. P53 does not change the P45/P46 queue lifecycle or any conductor, skill registry, head, minimap or Foot implementation. Normal explicit stop remains neutral and immediate.

Vehicle selection uses temporal box movement where camera motion permits a useful estimate, then bottom-of-frame proximity and passenger-car preference. Large vehicles do not gain preference merely by filling more pixels. Association is one-to-one, with center and scale gates, so changing detector order cannot casually transfer a blocked-door history between neighboring cars. Motion remains uncertain during substantial camera turns; these detections are not claimed to prove a stopped vehicle. A visible unclassified alternative is preferred to a known moving target. The reach and steering constants are provisional image-space estimates, not calibrated physical distances.
