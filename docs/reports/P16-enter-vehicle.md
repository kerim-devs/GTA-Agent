## Review

Checked in the night of 10 to 11 November.

The skill and the detector did not fit together. `Detection.box` is in pixels of the frame, as `contract.py` says and as the detector delivers it; the skill and its simulation took boxes as fractions of the picture and threw everything else away as invalid. On the console it would never have found a vehicle, while every test and all 1300 simulated runs passed. The skill converts on the way in now, and the tests and the simulation feed it pixels.

Three more changes. Triangle was pressed for one tick of 33 ms, which the console can miss, as in P21; it is held for 0.09 s. The argument is `duration` as in every other skill and the skill exposes its `limit`, which is where the runner looks to know how long a plan still runs; with `seconds` it would have assumed 120. And the wait after the press is 4 s instead of 3: the game walks the character to the door by itself after triangle and a moved stick cancels that walk, so a retry that comes early undoes the entry it is waiting for. That number is a guess in both versions. The time from the press to the tilted minimap is the first thing to measure on the console.

The simulation with these changes, 100 runs each: 98, 98 and 94 at half the field of view, 99, 99 and 98 at the assumed one, 91, 97 and 100 at double; `lab/vehicle_entry/results.json` is from this version. The simulated game lets triangle work only within 2.8 m and knows nothing of the walk to the door, so it punishes a longer wait and says little about the real one.

Eighteen breaks were tried and sixteen failed a test. The bound on the number of attempts was never reached because the time limit comes first, and a detection with low confidence was walked to; both are pinned now.

Open: nothing here has seen the game, and the skill is only as good as the detections it gets. The detector that finds near vehicles is still being built (P14b); without detections the skill ends after five seconds and says so. It is not registered yet.
