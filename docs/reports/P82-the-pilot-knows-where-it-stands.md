P82 adds a nonblocking place sense owned by the conductor. It uses only the dedicated where asker, submits at most one valid RGB frame per second, and keeps one outstanding request. Two agreeing answers from different captures establish room, landing or street. Answers with errors, a share below 0.6, or latency above two seconds break the agreement. Confirmations expire after five seconds and after two seconds away from foot gameplay. Requests, saved prompts and every play tick distinguish an unconfigured model (null), an unconfirmed model (unknown), and a confirmed place.

Free-play decisions now leave a room with leave_building for 60 seconds and descend from a landing for 40 seconds before errands, standing recovery or ordinary intent rules can send the figure outside. Room use steps remain available. Missions, blocking screens, death, wanted stars and low health retain their previous decisions. Unknown places wait for one second during the first six seconds from the first head observation; the clock is anchored there because live observation times are absolute monotonic timestamps. A known place remains available to the head for ten seconds after its last known request, without refreshing that hold from unknown requests. Disabling the model leaves place null and does not revive a held verdict.

The failure wording is interpreted as three attempts: the initial call, then attempts two and three preceded by look at 180 degrees for a room or 120 degrees for a landing. After the third failed call, the fourth decision uses the previous rules for 120 seconds. Failed result snapshots are deduplicated; fresh skill_failed events distinguish repeated calls with identical failure reasons. A completed call or a changed place resets the count and rest. The new state participates in MissionHead's discarded-decision rollback. The card's phrase "where a HUD is read" is interpreted as allowing the existing no-HUD observation path, while requiring screen game whenever a HUD exists.
