# P73: Driving like a player: quicker, never standing, and free again within seconds

## Why

Kerim on 13 November, on what has to get better next: the driving. "It does not have to drive by German traffic rules, this is Grand Theft Auto, don't forget that. It may drive over the pavement, wherever." What the pilot's drive did at the console on 11 and 12 November is in the log and in `docs/WALKTHROUGH.md`, lines 6 to 8: two kilometres in one go at about 9 m/s, a third of the time on the brake before the live settings (`cli.LIVE`), twice stood for 70 s short of its destination because the reader called every map damaged, and turning round or getting free of a wall "not measurable, turning not built". His own driving is measured (`docs/reports/P40-kerims-driving-as-the-measure.md`, and the escapes in `docs/reports/P68-getting-away.md`): the pedal fully down or not at all (R2 at 0.9 or more a median 69 % of the time), the brake on 4.5 % of the time, the stick in short full taps, 89 % of the time on a road and the rest across whatever lies there.

The plan's measure for the stream is that the story moves forward: the pilot may hit a lamp post, but it has to be free within about ten seconds and it must never stand.

Read `PLAN.md`, `docs/tasks/README.md`, the log's sections on driving (11 November "The first drive to a waypoint" and "Faster, and around what is in the road", 12 November "The basics, measured"), the reports P34, P36, P54, P55 and P68, then `pilot/src/pilot/drive.py`, `avoid.py`, `skills.py` (the class `DriveRoute` and `Skill._around`), `getaway.py`, `terrain.py`, `sim.py`, `cli.py` (`LIVE`, the settings the console asked for), `humandrive.py`, and the tools `tools/evaluate_driver.py`, `sim_console.py`, `human_drive.py` and `lab/drive/` (the recorded failures and recoveries, `replay.py`, `recovery.py`).

## What to do, in this order

Where the time goes. Over the recorded drives of the pilot at the console (the sessions under `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/` with `drive_route` in their `play.jsonl`; `lab/live/drivelog.py` and `lab/drive/replay.py` read them) make the table first: metres, seconds, mean speed, and the seconds spent standing, by cause (behind a vehicle, a map the reader flagged, a recovery under way, the pedal up for a bend, the brake, an arrival, a car that will not move, unknown). Beside it Kerim's own numbers on the same kind of road from P40's output. This table says what to change, and it is in the report before any change.

Never standing. Every cause of standing gets an answer that moves the car, and a bound in seconds after which the next answer comes. Behind a vehicle that stands: round it, on either side, over the kerb where that is the open side, after two seconds and not thirty (`queue_wait`), unless a red light is the plain reason and the cross traffic is in the detector's boxes. A flagged map: drive on by the last clean route and the road the minimap shows (`terrain.read` names where a road leaves the arrow) at a moderate speed instead of standing, and say `map-unreadable` only where neither is there for ten seconds. A recovery that did not free the car: the next one differs (the other lock, longer back, then forward at full lock), and the count of them is over time and distance, so that a car that was freed and drove a hundred metres starts afresh. A car on its roof or its side, burning or in water (the figure's vehicle box upside down or the map not moving with the pedal down and the recoveries spent; water under the arrow by `terrain`) ends the drive at once as `dead-or-held`, which the rules answer by getting out.

Turning round. A route that begins behind the car (the bearing more than 120 degrees off for a second) is not followed in a wide circle through whatever stands there: where the road is wide enough for the car's turning circle at low speed it turns on the spot with the handbrake (`r1` in the profile, check it), otherwise back with full lock and forward with the opposite one, as Kerim's recorded recoveries do (`lab/drive/human-recoveries.jpg`, `recovery.json`). Bounded at six seconds, and measured in the simulator on every car profile.

Quicker. With the console's settings the drive holds the car to a planned speed that a bend lowers long before it. A player keeps the pedal down, lifts for a sharp bend and steers early. Raise what the measurements carry: the straight-line limit, the lateral budget, how late the lift before a bend comes, no brake for speed below what a bend really needs. The pavement and the verge are road: a route that cuts a corner is cut, and the avoider may send the car over a kerb to pass. What stays: no input the picture does not justify, the pedal comes up on a picture that says nothing, and people are steered round, not braked for at speed and not driven at. Every change is a named setting with a comment that says where its number comes from, the present live values stay available as a style of their own (`careful`), the new ones are `bold`, `drive_route` takes the style as an argument with `bold` the default, and `cli.py`'s `LIVE` is the careful style under another name. If you need the argument registered in a file that is not yours, say so.

Arriving. A drive that ends at its place stops the car within a second and a half of the route going, off the middle of the road where the minimap shows the road's edge, and the report says how far from the place the car stands in the simulator. The figure will get out there and walk to the door.

`get_away` keeps to the road. The report of P68 found that turning away from the police's markers is not how Kerim gets away: he keeps to the road he is on, and where two markers lie on either side the skill's rule sends the car off sideways. Change the heading as that report proposes: the road that leads on within 45 degrees of straight ahead is kept whatever the markers say, a road is chosen away from the markers only at a junction where two lead on, and open ground is taken only where no road leads on. Run the comparison of its heading with Kerim's stick again (`lab/getaway/scripts/beside.py`) and give the new numbers beside the old.
