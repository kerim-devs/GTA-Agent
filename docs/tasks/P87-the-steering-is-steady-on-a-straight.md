# P87: The steering is steady on a straight

## Why

P84 measured the weaving drive of 13 November and mended nothing (`docs/reports/P84-the-drive-does-not-weave.md`, the scripts and numbers under `lab/drive/`; read both first). Its measure was the wrong one, and that was the card's fault: it counted how often the stick changes side on recorded readings replayed through the controller. The recorded readings carry the weave of the drive they were taken on, so a replay cannot show a drive that would not have weaved, and Kerim's own hand changes side five to seven times in a quarter minute and drives straight. What Kerim sees and objects to is how far the car swings, not how often the stick crosses the middle.

What P84 found stands, and it names the engine of the swing. The driver steers by the angle to `_target(path, lookahead_px)` with a look-ahead of 8 px. The reader gives no point of the path nearer than 11 px and the points lie about 6 px apart, so `_target` continues the first two points back to the 8 px circle: a segment of 6 px whose ends each wander by 0.6 to 0.8 px sets the direction, and the angle to the aim jumps by 14 degrees from one frame to the next at the 90th percentile, where the bearing the reader gives over a longer stretch jumps by 3.3. Then the steering law in `Driver.step` (from `previous = self._filtered_error` on): the error is smoothed over 0.1 s, 8 degrees of dead band are taken off, and whatever is left gets `steering_bias` on top, 0.25 of the stick at once. Nine degrees of error are 26.5 % of the stick, and `profile.shape` may already lift every stick value over the pad's own dead zone before it is sent, in which case the quarter is a real quarter of the steering and not the first movement of it. Check that. A law that is zero inside 8 degrees and a quarter of the lock outside is a relay, and a relay fed with an angle that jumps by 14 degrees swings the car: nineteen of twenty changes of side between 135 and 195 s follow within 0.35 s on the near angle crossing 8 degrees. The stick lay at 0.4 to 0.8 of full on average while the road was straight.

Kerim's order of 13 November: a sound base comes first, driving is the first part of it, and it has to run smoothly. Perfect is not asked for.

## What to do

First the measure. Find one that tells Kerim's drive from the pilot's on straight stretches, from the recordings P84 used (`/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/20261113-163218`, 120 to 315 s; `/Users/kerim/recordings/kerim/20261113-141634` and `kerim/20261113-031159`, all read-only) and more of Kerim's if they help: the mean and the root mean square of the stick, the spread of the car's heading against the direction of the route (the arrow against the chord of the path over the next 20 to 30 px), the size of the swings from one extreme of that angle to the next. A stretch counts as straight where the path ahead bends by less than a figure you state. Give the numbers for both drivers and say which measure you take and where between the two its limit lies. If no measure separates them, say so with the numbers and stop there: then the weave is not in the steering.

Then a closed loop that weaves. `lab/drive/simulate.py` drives the existing vehicle model with noise on the path's points. Make it show the pilot's swing with the controller as it is: the path given as the reader gives it (first point at 11 px or more, points 6 px apart, each wandering as measured), the delay you can support from P84's proxies (0.2 s from the angle to the stick, about 0.6 s until the map turns), the pad's dead zone as the profile shapes it. It is fit for the work when the unchanged controller on a straight road gives your measure in the range of the recording, and you say how near. A simulator in which the old controller drives straight proves nothing about a new one.

Then the steering, in `drive.py`, tried in that loop and only kept if it holds there. The leads, in the order I would try them, and you follow the numbers and not this list: a steering law without the jump, continuous from the edge of the dead band, or with a hysteresis so that the stick changes side only when the error has gone clearly to the other side and stayed; a further aim where the path ahead is straight, since a pixel of wander is 7 degrees at 8 px and 3 at 20, and the short aim kept where the path bends, because the short aim is there to keep the car from cutting a corner; a steadier direction of the first segment, from more than two points where they lie in a line. Speed is unknown on nine ticks of ten (729 of 776 between 150 and 180 s), so nothing may hang on it. Do not buy steadiness with lateness: on the simulator's left bend, right bend and S-bend the car must stay as near the road as it does now, within the existing limit of 5 m and not more than half a metre worse than before on any of them.
