# P70: Errands: what the pilot does when nothing is asked, and the way to the next mission

## Why

Kerim, in the early morning of 13 November: the aim is that GTA V plays its story by itself, with an agent that decides on its own, quickly, and is wrong in five to ten cases of a hundred at the most. In between it may do what a player does: go home and change, get a haircut, go into a clothes shop, get into a shootout, drive over the pavement. "You have to do that at random. The decisions have to be good, they have to be better. It must not get in its own way. It has to find the way there." And of the hour in the barber's the night before: how did it get in there at all, why does it walk along there, such things must not happen any more, and we cannot sit at one barber's for an hour.

What the pilot does today when nothing is asked is a fixed round in `pilot/src/pilot/goals.py`: take a vehicle, drive to a shop or pastime from the map's legend, get out and punch somebody, get away, and again. It never goes into the place it drove to, it takes a car for a trip of a hundred metres, it sets a waypoint with the police behind it, and the way to a mission letter is one rule among many in `mission.py` with nothing that follows the arrival. The Codex head behind the rules is no longer there to fall back on: the rules have to carry the game alone, and where they do not know, a small local model picks one of the choices they offer (card P74).

Read `PLAN.md`, `docs/tasks/README.md`, `docs/WALKTHROUGH.md`, the log of 12 and 13 November in `docs/LOG.md`, then `pilot/src/pilot/goals.py`, `mission.py`, `head.py`, `head_prompt.txt`, `conductor.py` (how a goal, an event and a plan travel), `skills.py` (the registry and what `SkillRunner.describe` reports, since 13 November with the running skill's `args`), `legend.py` (`kind_of`, the legend's names) and `notes.py` (`intent`). The reports P51, P52, P55 and P68 in `docs/reports/` say why the present rules are as they are; what they measured stays in force unless this card says otherwise.

## What to do

Errands. A round nobody asked for becomes an errand: one thing a player would go and do, from start to end, with a time limit and an end that was observed. In `goals.py` (a new module `errands.py` beside it for the tables and the choice, if that reads better) build these:

- `story`: to the nearest mission letter. `set_waypoint` with `target: mission`, the trip there, out of the car, `walk_to_point` with `exact: true` onto the letter. Reached when an objective or a cutscene shows after the arrival. A letter that starts nothing within twenty seconds of standing on it (another character's, a time of day, a call that has not come) is remembered for the session and the next try takes another (`instance`), and after two letters that started nothing `switch_character` is planned once before the next.
- `shop`: to a place of the legend that can be gone into on foot: the barber's (`Friseursalon`), the clothes shops (`Binco Bekleidung`, `Discount Store`, `Suburban`, `Ponsonbys`), `Ammu-Nation` and the `Tattoo-Studio`. The trip, out of the car, `walk_to_point` with `exact: true, enter: true`, then `use_place`, then `leave_building`. Reached when `leave_building` ends `outdoors` after a `use_place` that ended done; a `use_place` that fails `no-prompt` is still followed by `leave_building`.
- `home`: the same at the character's safehouse (`Unterschlupf`), where `use_place` changes clothes at the wardrobe. Never a bed and never a save: the save game is Kerim's.
- `mayhem`: what the round does today as `fight` and `escape`: `punch` somebody in the street and get away from what follows.
- `cruise`: what the round does today as `drive` to a point `away`.

The trip is one piece of logic for all of them. The waypoint is set first, since `set_waypoint` reports the distance (`waypoint set: <place> (<n>m)`). Kerim walks everything up to about 200 m round the corner: at 250 m or less the errand walks (`walk_to_point`, sprinting), further off it takes a vehicle (`enter_vehicle`, or `walk_road` until one is in view) and follows the route (`drive_route`). Arrived by car, the figure gets out, and since the game has taken the waypoint away by then, the waypoint is set again on the same place before the walk to its door. A trip that will not work is given up as the round does today (a held car is left, a drive that stalls twice on the way to a place ends the errand), and a place given up twice is not chosen again in the session.

The choice of the next errand is weighted and seeded like the round's (`Goals.random`): `story` 5, `shop` 3, `mayhem` 2, `cruise` 1, `home` 1 to begin with, as a table at the top of the module with a line of comment each. The same sort of shop not twice within thirty minutes, `home` once an hour, no `mayhem` within ten minutes of the last and none directly after an escape, `story` at half its weight after a letter that started nothing until a mission has run, and never the same errand twice in a row except `story`. `Goals` takes an optional `chooser`: a callable that is given the candidates with their weights and a short English description each and returns one of them or None; with None, or no chooser, the seeded draw decides. That is where the local model is plugged in later; nothing here waits for it or depends on it.

Every errand has a deadline (the table `LIMITS`) and counts its tries; at the limit it is given up and the next is chosen. The conductor's `goal` dictionary and `head.parse_response` are extended for the new names, and nothing else about their shape changes.

With stars lit nobody opens the map. In `mission.py` every rule that answered stars or an order to escape in a vehicle with `set_waypoint ... away` plus `drive_route` now plans `get_away` (the skill of P68, registered since 13 November); on foot with stars it is `enter_vehicle` and then `get_away`, and the sprint where no vehicle is in view, as today. A `get_away` that fails `vehicle-held` is answered by leaving the car and taking another; `water` and `on-foot` by the rules on foot.

Not in its own way. Today every event makes the rules plan afresh, and a plan that begins with the very call that is running replaces it with a new instance of itself: the walk stops and turns its camera again, the driver forgets what it knew. A plan whose first call has the name and the arguments of the skill that is running (`last_skill` with status `running` and its `args`) is answered with `go_on`, unless the events of the request hold a failure, a screen, a `standing` or a guard event. Measure on the recorded sessions how often this happened before (`head.jsonl` of the sessions under `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/`: answers with disposition `replaced` whose plan begins with the skill that was running) and say so in the report.

Standing. The conductor will send a new event, `{"kind": "standing", "seconds": s, "count": n}`, when the figure has not moved for twelve seconds on the game's own screen with a plan running or none, `n` counting such events in a row within the errand (the reviewer builds the sender). The rules answer it at once and differently each time: first Square and a sprint straight on for three seconds; second a look of 120 degrees and a sprint of four seconds; third backwards for three seconds, a look of 180 and `walk_road`; in a vehicle the first is `drive_route` again when a route shows and otherwise out of the car; the fourth gives the errand up and begins another. A figure indoors (the last walk ended `entered`, or the errand is in its `use_place` step) is answered with `leave_building` from the second on.

Prompts. Where an objective is on the screen and its class is `use`, `pick_up` or `unknown`, and the help text asks for a button (it begins with or contains `Drücke` or `Halte`), the rules plan `do_prompt` once before whatever else they would do, and not again for the same help text.

The new skills (`use_place`, `do_prompt`, `leave_building`, `switch_character`, `walk_to_point` with `enter`) are in the registry with their names, arguments and prompts and fail `not-built` until their own cards are done. Plan with them as their prompts describe them; what they end on is in each prompt.
