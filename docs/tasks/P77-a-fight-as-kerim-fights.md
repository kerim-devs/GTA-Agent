# P77: A fight as Kerim fights it: the lock first, in his rhythm, and not standing in the open

## Why

`fight` has never run at the console. It turns the camera to the nearest red marker with the right stick until the marker is within five degrees ahead, and only then holds L2 and fires. On the reaction bench that left most enemy scenes with a turn of the stick and no shot (`docs/reports/P65-the-guard.md`: "Fight aims only where a marker happens to be within 5 degrees ahead"). Kerim, who plays with the game's full aim assist, on 12 November: L2 first, also when shooting; L2 targets the opponent and always helps. The game's lock does the turning: L2 pressed snaps the aim to an enemy in view, R2 fires, L2 let go and pressed again takes the next one. The same day he asked that what the game does by itself is asked of him and timed on his recordings before anything is estimated, and for punching that was done (`punch.py`: his presses last 0.12 s and follow each other 0.21 s apart). For shooting it was not. And on 13 November: the pilot may get into a shootout, that is the game.

His recordings hold his fights: 47 hits on him are labelled in `lab/health/cases.json` with the recording and the second, the reaction bench has 20 enemy scenes with their onsets (`lab/reaction/scenes.json`), and `/Users/kerim/recordings/derived/kerim/<name>/actions.jsonl` names the stretches in which he aims, fires, punches or is in cover, with `pad.jsonl` of the recording beside it for every press.

Read `PLAN.md`, `docs/tasks/README.md`, `docs/WALKTHROUGH.md` (lines 11 to 16), the log of 12 November ("The first punch at the console", "Ways of control, measured on 55 scenes"), `pilot/src/pilot/combat.py`, `punch.py`, `guard.py` (which starts `Fight` for a red marker and takes `nearest_red` from `combat.py`), `hits.py`, `reaction.py` and `approaches.py` with `tools/reaction.py` (the bench that replays a scene against a way of control), `tools/sim_combat.py` and the reports P18, P23, P62, P64, P65 and P66.

## What to do, in this order

How Kerim shoots. From his recordings, over every stretch in which he fires with a red marker on the map: how long after the marker first shows he presses L2, how long he holds it, how long after L2 the first R2 comes, how long a burst of R2 lasts and how long the gap is, how often he lets L2 go and presses it again and after how many bursts, what the right stick does in the half second before L2 and while it is held (does he turn first, or let the lock turn), what the left stick does while he fires (standing, strafing, backing away), how often and when he takes cover (R1) and reloads, and what he does when he is hit: in the two seconds after each of the 47 labelled hits. The same for the stretches in which he fires with no red marker on the map, apart. A table in the report, with the medians and the spread, and the count of fights it rests on.

`fight`, rebuilt on that. L2 first: with a red marker on the map the skill holds L2 at once, whichever way the camera looks, and fires in Kerim's rhythm once the lock has had the time his own first shot takes after L2. It lets L2 go and presses it again after his number of bursts, which takes the next enemy. The right stick turns the camera toward the nearest marker only while L2 is up between two locks, or when a lock has brought no end to the marker's being there for three locks in a row (the enemy is behind the figure, out of the lock's reach). It does not stand in the open while it is being hit: on a hit (`hits.Hits`, fed the HUD it is handed) it moves as he moves in his two seconds after one, sideways or back with the left stick while the lock holds, or into cover with `cover: yes`. Health under a third ends it as today, for the head's rules and the guard to flee. Without a red marker for two seconds it ends done as today. With stars lit it never fires: it lets go and ends failed `stars`, since the police are not to be shot at by any rule of the pilot.

What it cannot know. Whether a weapon is in the hand: nothing reads it. Find out on frames what the game draws when a weapon is drawn (the ammunition numbers at the top right appear when the figure is armed and are gone when it is not) and whether the text reader or a small reading of that corner can tell, on at least forty frames of each kind from his recordings. If it can, the reading goes into a module of yours as `armed(frame)` returning True, False or None, `fight` begins with it, and unarmed it ends failed `no-weapon` at once so that the rules can punch instead. If it cannot, say what was tried.

Held against the bench. Run the rebuilt skill through the reaction bench on the 20 enemy scenes behind the guard (`pilot+guard`, as P65 did), and give its rows beside P65's: on how many scenes it aims within a second of the onset and on how many it fires, where P65's turned the stick and never aimed. A replay never turns the camera and never locks, so say what the numbers can and cannot show. And in `tools/sim_combat.py`'s world, with a lock modelled as the game's (L2 held snaps to an enemy within its cone, as far as his recordings show the cone), the time from a marker to the first shot on target, old skill against new, enemies ahead, to the side and behind.

`melee` shares the code and keeps its behaviour; where the rhythm of `punch.py` contradicts its constants, `punch.py` is right.
