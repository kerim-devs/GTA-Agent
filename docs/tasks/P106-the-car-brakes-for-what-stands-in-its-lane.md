# P106: The car brakes for what stands in its lane

## Why

The trip of P97 arrives in town now (fifteen arrivals in the night of 14 November), and what ends or spoils most of the drives that do not is the same thing: the car drives into the back of what stands in its lane. The driver follows the route on the minimap and sees nothing of the road; `avoid.py` looks at the detector's boxes and bends the driver's command in `DriveRoute._around` (`skills.py`). It pushes the stick and lifts a share of the pedal by how near a box is, and it brakes (`AROUND_BRAKE`, 0.6) only for a thing with nearness 0.8 or more straight ahead, a few metres before the bumper. It knows neither the car's speed nor how fast a box grows beyond the yes or no of `closing()`, and it leaves out everything farther than `FAR`, five of the own car's distances, which is about twenty metres: at 22 m/s that is under a second.

`/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/20261114-035459`, by day. At 273.2 s the car is on a straight at 22 m/s with the pedal at 1.0 and a queue stands at the junction ahead. `around_pedal` in `play.jsonl` goes 0.68, 0.55, 0.30, 0.58 over the next second, the stick is pushed to the right, the pedal sent stays between 0.55 and 0.79, and no brake is sent until 274.7 s; at 275 s the car lies in a row of standing cars and needs a recovery. At 297.3 s of the same drive, 21.8 m/s on a straight, it runs into the back of a lorry, stands on its nose, and the figure is out of the car at 303 s and dead at 330 s. `20261114-034036`, by night: from 51 to 119 s of the trip the car is in a pile of cars it drove into, 68 s of bypass and three failed recoveries. `20261114-034630`: from 68 to 82 s it pushes the car before it at a red light with the pedal at 0.9 to 1.0 and then a police car, which lit a star and cost the next trip. `20261114-030418` (earlier in the night): 23 s against a garbage truck.

Kerim's order for the driving is that the car drives through and does not brake without a reason ("Er soll durchfahren. Es gibt keinen Grund, dass er bremst"). A standing lorry in the lane is a reason; a parked car beside a bend is not.

## What to do

Measure first and write it down. `play.jsonl` does not hold the detections, only `around_pedal` in each tick's `result`. Run the detector (`objects.Detector`, the model the pilot uses) over the video of the approaches above, at the interval the live reader runs it, and record for each: from which second and at what box height the thing that was hit has a box, how the box grows up to the impact, what `Avoider.step` returned, and the car's speed (`reading.gps_speed`) beside it. Say how far ahead a car and a lorry are found by day and by night, in seconds before contact at the speed driven, and where the detector loses a thing that is close (the own car hides the lower part of it).

Then give the avoider what a driver has: the time until the car is at the thing. The growth of a box gives it without any distance (the time to contact is the box's height over its growth per second), the car's speed and the distance by the box's size give it a second way; use what the recordings support and say how each errs. A thing in the car's own lane that it will reach sooner than it can stop is braked for at once and hard enough to stop before it, at any distance the detector sees it, not from `FAR` on; with the speed unknown the growth alone decides. Where a side is free the car still goes round, as today, but slowed to a speed at which the push on the stick gets it round. Behind a vehicle that drives, the car keeps its distance as P96 made it; behind one that stands it stops and then waits or passes as P101 made it, without first touching it.

The brake's strength needs the game's own figure: take the deceleration the car reaches under L2 from the recordings (the speed's fall in the seconds after a brake pulse, by day and in rain if both are there) and state it with its spread.

Count what it costs. On clean stretches of the same and of two other drives (`20261114-040426` from 91 to 168 s, `20261114-024817`, `20261114-005357` from 400 to 520 s on the freeway) the brakes the avoider asks for, per minute, before and after, each new one with its frame: a brake for a parked car beside a bend or for oncoming traffic in the other lane is a fault to be counted, not explained away.
