## What to do

Build the trip as the one order of a session. `pilot play --task trip --place <name of a place in the map's legend>` holds it from the first request to its verdict, and with it `MissionHead` answers every request from the trip alone: not from the rule ladder, the standing rule, the place rule, the draw of errands or the model behind the rules. Without `--task trip` the pilot behaves as it does today.

The trip has four steps and a destination that is fixed when it begins and never changes: a car, the waypoint, the drive, arrived. A step ends on what was observed and on nothing else. The car is had when the seat is confirmed, by `enter_vehicle` ending with the figure in a vehicle (it also ends so at once for a figure already seated, which is how a session that begins in a car gets through this step). The waypoint is set when `set_waypoint` ends done, names the place that was asked for, and the game's own screen is back. The drive has arrived when `drive_route` ends arrived for the waypoint this trip set: the route was seen, it is gone, and the printed distance is fresh and within the skill's arrival distance. Then the pad is neutral, the verdict is written, and the session ends.

Give every skill the runner starts a number of its own, and carry that number and the skill's arguments in what `describe()` reports for a running and for an ended skill, with the evidence the skill's result holds (the end distance and its age, the route seen). The trip moves on only for an ended attempt whose number it has not counted yet. Nothing is told apart by comparing dictionaries or by counting requests, and two attempts that end with the same words are two attempts.

A time limit ends a stretch and not the step. A `drive_route` that runs out of its seconds while the printed distance has fallen is followed by the next stretch of the same drive and costs no try. Progress belongs to the step and outlasts the skill: for the drive it is the printed distance to the destination, and a drive that has not come nearer for a time you state and justify (the report proposes 20 to 30 s for a review, the drive's own guards act sooner) goes to its recovery, in this order: on along the road where the route is not shown, then out of this car and into another with the destination kept, then the end of the trip as failed. For the car, the tries differ from one another: the nearest car first, then a turn and a look, then toward the road and again, within a budget of time and tries you state; a car seen a moment ago is walked to, not away from. For the waypoint, at most three tries, and before each the game's screen has to be confirmed; a try that leaves a map open is followed first by getting back to the game.

Decide what may interrupt, and let nothing else. A death or an arrest does not end the trip: when the game is back it begins again at the car with the same destination, at most twice. Kerim's hand on the controller suspends it, and when the pad is taken again the seat is checked and the trip goes on at its step. Stars, an objective text, low health, a report of standing, a quiet request and a plan that is ending are news the trip may use, and none of them replaces its plan or ends it; today `goals.interruption` and the conductor stop the runner for the first three, which must not happen to a trip. The whole trip has one budget, `--seconds` or 900, and ends as failed with the reason named when it is spent.

Open the two locks on a map. `Flow.tick` returns for a damaged reading before it has looked at the screen the HUD reader names, and `Conductor.tick` drops every newly pressed button on a damaged reading. Under an open map the minimap is not there to be read, so the back button never arrives: recording `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/20261113-202400`, requests 4 to 17, screen map, minimap none, corrupt true, fifteen waypoint tries in a row. A menu the HUD reader names on a fresh frame is reason enough for the button that leaves it. The protection of a damaged picture of the game itself stays as it is, and you show both.

Make the log say what happened: the trip begun with its destination, each step begun, each attempt ended with its number, status, reason and evidence, the trip suspended and taken up again, and the verdict, reached or failed with its reason, in `head.jsonl` in a form one small script can count. The session ends within a few seconds of the verdict, prints it, and leaves with exit code 0 for reached and another for failed.

Add `lab/live/trip.sh [place] [seconds]` beside `story.sh`, with the same checks before any input (the bridge answers, the game is in the picture, no hand on the controller, no human recording running), the judge given as `story.sh` gives it, no guard.

What stays as it is: the driving itself (`drive.py`, `avoid.py`, how `drive_route` steers and brakes), the inside of `enter_vehicle` and `set_waypoint`, the errands and everything a session without `--task trip` does. If a test shows that the trip cannot end without a change inside one of those skills, make the smallest change that does it and say so in the report; do not tune them. `movement()` in `mission.py` takes an unknown word with a tilted map for a car: the trip must not depend on it, and you leave it alone.
