# P108: The turn begins in time

## Why

At a junction the car holds the stick in the middle until it is in the junction, and then turns with the full stick. With the turn speeds of this morning's trial (`junction_speed=9 turn_speed=9 lateral_acceleration=5 coast_loss=4 speed_lag=1.6 speed_limit=22`, given to the pilot with `--set`) that is too late: the car leaves the carriageway on the far side.

Recording `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/20261114-072659` (read-only), two turns of one trip.

At 157 to 165 s, a turn to the left into Vinewood Boulevard. The speed law took the car from 17.6 to 5 m/s by 161 s, in good time. From 158.6 s to 164.0 s the stick stood at 0.0 (but for 0.7 s at 161 s) while the route's near bearing went from 24 to 64 degrees to the left, and from 161 s the pedal went to 0.75 and then 1.0 to bring the car to the turn's speed. The path read at 162.0 s begins `(-3,13) (-9,19)`, at 162.9 s `(-7,13) (-13,15)`, at 163.4 s `(-9,11) (-17,11)`, at 163.8 s `(-12,7) (-19,7)`. The heading rule in `_target` (`radius <= ahead <= hypot(x, y) + HIDDEN_SLACK and before <= 0`) put the meeting point of the car's heading with the first segment 10, 10.7 and 11 px ahead and aimed straight on; at 163.8 s it was 7 px, under the short aim's 8, and the stick went to -0.52 and -0.98 within half a second, at a read speed of 10 m/s. At 165 s the car stood on the far pavement among people, drove into a shop's entrance, stayed there for 100 s through `turn-join`, `turn-clear`, `turn-along` and two recoveries, got two stars and was shot.

At 99.8 to 103.0 s, a turn to the right: the stick at 0.0 for 3.2 s while the near bearing went from 39 to 92 degrees and the car drove 23 m by the printed distance (659 to 636 m) at 3 to 9 m/s with the pedal at 0.7 to 0.9. The path's first point went round the car at 13 px, `(0,12)`, `(4,13)`, `(9,12)`, `(11,7)`, `(12,5)`, `(12,1)`, and the meeting point stayed 10 to 12 px ahead the whole time. A corner that stands does not stay ahead of a car that drives 23 m towards it on a map of 0.7 to 1 m to the pixel. The full stick came at 103.6 s in the middle of the junction, `turn-join` at 104.4 s.

So there are two things to tell apart, and I have not: the distance at which the turn begins is 8 px whatever the speed, 5.6 to 8 m on the map of a slow car, where a car at 10 m/s with the stick's delay needs about 12; and the meeting point the rule reckons from the first visible segment may not be the corner at all while the car is already in the bend. Over the nineteen trips from 06:00 to 07:36 the car went into `turn-join` after following in a bend eleven times in 131 turns of sixty degrees and more; my first count (`lab/drive/p108_idle_stick.py`, rough, it counts an onset again after each tick of coasting) gives 23 onsets with the stick idle for a second and more while the route lay thirty degrees and more to a side, with repeats.

Kerim's order is that the car drives through and is not slow; his own turns at junctions are taken at 14 to 21 m/s. The turn speed stays where the trial has it or goes up. The turn has to begin earlier.

P95 (`docs/tasks/P95-the-short-aim-before-a-corner.md` and its report) is why the short aim is 8 px and why the heading rule exists: read both first. Taking the short aim further out is not the answer by itself: at 10 px the planar simulator's hidden bend is cut and 5 cases of `tests/test_sim.py` and `tests/test_gps.py` failed, at 12 px 17. `HIDDEN_SLACK` and the note above it in `_target` are from this night (`05ed1bc`), the aim under `hidden-turn` in `_road_limit` from `2ba117c`.
