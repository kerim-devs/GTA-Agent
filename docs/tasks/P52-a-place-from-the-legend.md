# P52: A place from the map's legend

## Why

When the pilot wants to drive somewhere it sets a waypoint by pushing the cursor over the pause map and checking in the picture that the map moved. In town by day on 12 November that failed nine times and worked once (`player-marker-unreadable`, `map-shift-unverified`, `waypoint-not-visible-after-cross` in `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/20261112-102851` and `20261112-103305`), and where it works the target is "away": some point far off. Kerim's word on it the same morning: a goal on the map has to be a real one, a mission or a place, not a click somewhere, and the list at the right of the map is the easy way to pick one, be it a mission, the barber or a shop.

The list was tried by hand through the bridge, recording `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/20261112-104238` (92 s; `lab/legend/readings.json` holds what the native text reader reads in it twice a second, `lab/legend/*.jpg` three of its frames). The presses were: options, which opens the pause menu on its tab KARTE; cross, which opens the map with the legend at the right, 19 entries in view of 26, the first one, "Spieler", selected, and a counter "1/26" under it; pad down, which selects the next entry, shows "‹ 2/9 ›" beside it (the second of nine places of that kind) and centres the map on that place; nine more times down to "Friseursalon ‹ 6/7 ›", counter "11/26"; cross, which sets the waypoint there: the route is drawn in purple and the legend gains an entry "Wegpunkt (532m)"; circle twice, back in the game with the purple route on the minimap. No cursor is moved and nothing has to be read off the map's picture, by day or by night. The footer names the buttons: "Markierung wählen" on the pad, "Wegpunkt" on cross, "Zurück" on circle.

The text reader reads the legend as it is: every entry by its name, the selected one with its arrows as "Friseursalon < 6/7 > q", the counter as "11/28" for "11/26", a symbol behind a name as a stray letter.

Read `PLAN.md`, in `docs/LOG.md` the entries on the waypoint (11 November) and from "On foot" on, the reports of P19 and P35, `pilot/src/pilot/waypoint.py` with its tests, `pilot/src/pilot/scene.py` (`TextReader`), `pilot/src/pilot/skills.py` (`Press` and how a skill presses a button so that the game takes it once), `pilot/src/pilot/head_prompt.txt`, and how the conductor treats a skill that is `uninterruptible`.

## What to build

`set_waypoint` with a place: `{"place": "Friseursalon"}`, the name as the legend prints it, or `{"kind": "shop"}`, `{"kind": "mission"}` and the like for a choice among the entries that are there. The skill opens the map as above, reads the legend, steps to the entry and sets the waypoint, and it knows at each step where it is from what it reads, never from counting presses: the selected entry is the one with the arrows, the counter says which of how many, a press that did not take is pressed again, and a legend that scrolls is followed. When a kind has several places, it takes the one the game offers first unless told which. It is done when the entry "Wegpunkt" with its distance stands in the legend, and it then leaves the map and waits for the game's picture; the distance and the name of the place go into its result, so that the head can say where it is going. It fails with a reason, and with the map closed again, when the place is not in the legend, when the menu is not the map, when nothing it presses moves the selection, and when the time runs out. It never presses anything while it does not read the pause menu or the map.

The kinds: sort the entries the legend can show into missions (a name or letter of a person, "Fremde und Freaks"), shops and services (barber, clothes, weapons, the garage for tuning, tattoos), pastimes (cinema, golf, the cable car, races) and what is no destination (the player, the waypoint itself). Take the entries from the recording and from the frames of Kerim's play in which he has the map open (`/Users/kerim/recordings/derived/kerim/*/sections.json` marks menus; `20261111-205309` from 375 s and `20261111-210812` from 486 s hold minutes of it), and let an entry nobody has listed fall under a kind of its own that is never chosen unasked.

The old way stays for the target "away" and for the mission letter where the legend has nothing, and the rules of the head are not yours: say in the report which call the rules should make instead of "away", and how the list of places the skill has read can reach the head.
