# The left stick that jumps by itself

Kerim said from the 12th on that the figure and the car pull to the left, and on the night to 13 November that his recordings show a swerve to the left again and again, out of nothing. It is the controller. In every recording of his play since 11 November the left stick's x jumps to the left for a few hundredths of a second, mostly to the full -1.0, from a stick at rest or held forward, with y not moving: 1128 times under 80 ms in the 80 minutes recorded that night, against five to the right, and the right stick shows none. One or two a minute on the 11th, two to nine on the 12th, ten to eighteen on the 13th. A restart of the console or the Mac does not change a stick; another controller does.

`spikes.py count` prints the line per recording. `spikes.py clean --apply RECORDING ...` writes the controller log without them and keeps the log as recorded beside it as `pad.raw.jsonl`; the rule and what it was measured on stand at the top of the file. On 13 November it took 2845 such excursions, 143 s in all, out of the logs of all 44 recordings under `/Users/kerim/recordings/kerim/`; 306 longer pulls to the left that begin with the same jump were left in and are listed as doubtful in each `pad.spikes.json`. `cleaned-20261113.txt` is the list. The pictures are as they were: the game answered what it was sent, so a car that twitched still twitches in the video. Whatever was derived from the logs before (`derived/kerim/`) has to be derived again, and `pilot/tools/trim.py check` against an untrimmed source will name the changed lines.

`raw.py [seconds]` prints what the controller at this computer reports before the bridge's rest zone, for a test with the stick let go or held against its rim (run it with `remoteplay/.venv/bin/python`).

The keep-awake script's own turns of the camera are a second thing: 21 of them went into his recordings on 12 and 13 November, half a second each, in moments when he had let the controller go. They are in the logs as `src: agent`, listed in `pad.spikes.json`, and since 03:43 on the 13th the script waits twelve minutes after his last input while a recording runs.
