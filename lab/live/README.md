# Looking at a live session

Small tools from the live rounds of 11 November. Each takes the folder of a session under `/Users/kerim/recordings/pilot/` and is run with `pilot/.venv/bin/python`.

`log.py` prints the skills of a session in order with how each ended, what went to the console and what the judge said. `drivelog.py` prints pedals, route readings, the distance to the waypoint over time and what the head was asked. `sheet.py <session> <out.jpg> <seconds>...` puts the frames at those seconds side by side. `boxes.py <session> <seconds>...` prints the boxes the object detector finds there. `yaw.py <session> <from> <to>...` measures from the picture how far the camera turned in each span; the first span has to be more than a full circle unless `CIRCLE` gives the pixels of one (2244 at a width of 640 with the far camera).

`record-kerim.sh` runs beside the bridge and records Kerim's own play to `/Users/kerim/recordings/kerim/`: it starts when his controller reports input through the bridge, cuts a running recording every twenty minutes and stops after ten quiet ones.

A round at the console: the bridge runs as `remoteplay/.venv/bin/ps5rp --host <console> --fps 60 daemon`; before every run the picture is looked at (`ps5rp snapshot`) and `/status` on port 9395 must say that nobody is on the controller and nothing records. Then `pilot play --seconds N --judge http://<server>:1234=qwen/qwen3-vl-8b` with `--plan '<json>'` for one plan or `--head rules-only` for the rules.

`story.sh [seconds] [bold|careful]` is the one command for a session in which the pilot plays the story by itself: it checks that the bridge has a session, that nobody is on the controller and nothing records, asks whether the local model answers and passes it on only then, and runs `pilot play --head local --guard` with the driving style given. A hand on the controller during the session is not the end of it: the pilot puts its plan down and goes on when the controller is let go of.

`play-session.sh [console address]` is the one command for a long session of Kerim's own play: it starts the bridge if none runs and records without a break, a new folder every fifteen minutes, until Ctrl-C, whether or not a controller hangs on this Mac. Both scripts give up when less than 4 GB are left on the disk; an hour of play is about 2.8 GB. It belongs in a terminal of his own, because an agent's session ends its background tasks after two hours. `record-kerim.sh` is the other way round: it runs beside a bridge that is already up and records only while his controller reports input through it.

`replay_drive.py <session> [name=value...]` runs the driver again over the readings of a session with the live settings and prints its states, pedals and speed limits; that is how the limit stuck at its slowest value was found. `avoid_sheet.py <session> <out.jpg> <seconds>...` draws frames with every box of the detector and what the avoider makes of them. `damage_count.py <session> <from> <to>` counts the frames of a stretch that the minimap reader calls damaged.
