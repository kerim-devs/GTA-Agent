This is the P14 object-detector benchmark, not an accepted model. `labels.json` contains 80 manually inspected frames selected with Python seed 1414: 24 from each clean cable recording and 32 from the long Wi-Fi recording. Annotation used raw frames without detector predictions. Boxes describe visible extents in original 1280x720 pixels; `player` distinguishes the character and ridden vehicle. Pickups and delivery box vans are trucks, SUVs and passenger cars are cars, and coaches/RVs are buses. The last annotation pass corrected five pickup classes. Damaged images remain in the sample and have a flag; the nearly black frame is retained with an empty label list.

Every labelled vehicle wider than 60 pixels and person taller than 40 pixels is scored. Borderline smaller labels are retained as ignore objects, and smaller unmatched predictions are not scored because those objects were not exhaustively annotated. Matching is one-to-one in descending confidence, requires the same kind and IoU at least 0.5, and counts duplicates as inventions. The large-object recall uses ground-truth vehicle width greater than 100 or person height greater than 80. Player leakage is any retained prediction overlapping a player box at IoU 0.5, irrespective of class, divided by all 80 frames. Filter collateral is any removed prediction overlapping a non-player label at IoU 0.5, irrespective of class. These measurements share the frames used to choose the camera-relative filter; there is no independent holdout or claim about unseen camera positions.
